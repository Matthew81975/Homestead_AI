HCS-AI 0.11.0 — Native Local Codex
===================================

- Integrates Local Codex 2.10.8 as an HCS-owned worker instead of a separate
  terminal application.
- Adds a full Local Codex tab with workspace selection, prompts, task controls,
  approvals, status, and a searchable live log.
- Copies compatible standalone state during a safe, one-time migration. The
  standalone installation remains untouched for rollback.
- Supervises crashes, heartbeat loss, cooperative stopping, and full worker
  process-tree cleanup on HCS exit or restart.
- Preserves Local Codex workspace and action safety boundaries and keeps
  credentials out of tracked configuration, migration receipts, and logs.

Known first-release limit: Local Codex runs one task at a time. Submit another
task after the active task completes, stops, or becomes resumable.
