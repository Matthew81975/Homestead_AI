HCS-AI v0.7 — Self-Contained Local AI + HKR
============================================
1. Extract this ZIP to a permanent folder.
2. Double-click install.bat.
3. The installer creates Desktop and Start Menu shortcuts and launches HCS-AI.
4. Accept the optional Internal AI download during setup (about 2.5 GB for the model).
5. HCS-AI starts and stops llama.cpp automatically; LM Studio is not required.
6. Use System > Internal AI Engine to select another GGUF model or control the engine.
7. Use the HKR Librarian tab to research and add expert knowledge.
