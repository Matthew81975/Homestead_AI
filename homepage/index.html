🧪 Quick Growing Rules
TopicRule of thumb
Seed depthPlant about 2–3 times as deep as the seed is thick; surface-sow very tiny seed.
Soil readinessSoil should crumble rather than smear or form a wet ribbon when squeezed.
Transplant hardeningGradually expose seedlings to sun, wind and cooler nights for 7–10 days.
WateringTarget roughly 1 inch per week for many garden crops, adjusted for heat, soil and crop stage.
Succession plantingSow fast crops every 1–3 weeks instead of planting the entire season’s supply at once.
RotationAvoid returning the same plant family to the same bed for at least 3 years when practical.
Seed storageKeep seed cool, dark and dry in sealed containers; label variety and year.
Days to maturityFor transplanted crops, packet days usually start at transplanting; for direct-sown crops, at sowing.
